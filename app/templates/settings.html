Wiring the indicator (auto mode)

1. Cable the weighbridge indicator's RS232 port to the office PC (USB-serial adapter).
2. On that PC run: python scale_reader.py --site quarry --port COM3 (Windows) or --port /dev/ttyUSB0 (Linux).
3. First time, add --raw to see the raw frames and match the parser to your indicator model.
4. Flip the mode above to Auto indicator — the ticket page then shows a live readout and the operator locks the stable weight.
5. No reader running? The page says so and manual entry keeps working — weighing never stops.
